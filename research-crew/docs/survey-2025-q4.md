# Internal survey: AI coding assistants (fictional sample data for the demo)
Published: 2025-11. Method: survey of 420 engineers at 12 mid-size companies.

## Adoption
- 62% of engineers use an AI coding assistant every week.
- 18% use one for code review.

## Reported impact
- Engineers estimate they save 3.1 hours per week.
- 41% say they trust AI-written code less than their own.
